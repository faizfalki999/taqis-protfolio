GRAPHIC DESIGN

GATORACING

Heritage motorsports graphic design, poster graphics, and apparel insignia capturing retro speed culture, raw grit, and Florida swamp raceway folklore.

CATEGORY Graphic Design / Apparel Art
ROLE Graphic Design & Vector Art
TIMELINE 2025
TOOLS Illustrator, Photoshop
ORIGINAL EMBLEM (LIGHT CANVAS)
MONOCHROME INVERTED (STREETWEAR MOCKUP)

DESIGN CONCEPT & STORY

Gatoracing merges the raw aggression of vintage Florida drag strips with refined modern streetwear vector styling. Developed as a multi-format graphic design project suitable for oversized heavyweight streetwear tees, screenprinted poster art, and custom die-cut vinyl stickers.

The circular arch balance anchors the dynamic hand-vectored gator illustration in the center, framing the custom curved typography with high optical balance and timeless mid-century motorsport aesthetics.

APPLICATION SPECS

  • Discipline: Graphic Design, Screenprint Art & Vector Emblems
  • Geometry: True concentric circle radial grid layout
  • Typography: Custom modified geometric grotesk display
  • Primary Use: Streetwear apparel, screenprinted posters & automotive decals
