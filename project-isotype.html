ISOTYPE

VORTEX PINWHEEL

Mathematical 8-segment rotational turbine mark utilizing negative space curvature, engineered for kinetic motion design, audio tech identities, and futuristic sub-labels.

CATEGORY Kinetic Isotype
ROLE Geometric Mark & Motion Design
TIMELINE 2025
TOOLS Illustrator, After Effects
ORIGINAL ISOTYPE (LIGHT CANVAS)
MONOCHROME INVERTED (STREETWEAR MOCKUP)

DESIGN CONCEPT & STORY

Vortex Pinwheel is a dynamic study in rotational geometry. By offsetting the curvature of eight organic teardrop blades around a central circular nucleus, the mark delivers an optical illusion of continuous infinite rotation even in static 2D print form.

When brought into motion, the geometry easily translates into spinning loaders, kinetic audio visualizers, and looping 3D brand idents.

APPLICATION SPECS

  • Discipline: Rotational Geometric Isotype
  • Symmetry: 8-fold rotational radial symmetry (45° intervals)
  • Motion: Designed natively for 60fps infinite spin loops
  • Primary Use: Audio tech hardware, digital app icons & kinetic branding
