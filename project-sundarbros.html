MONOGRAM

SUNDAR BROS.

Heavyweight geometric stadium monogram badge combining mid-century industrial manufacturing marks with bold modern luxury streetwear branding.

CATEGORY Monogram & Emblem
ROLE Monogram Design & Typography
TIMELINE 2024
TOOLS Illustrator
ORIGINAL MONOGRAM (LIGHT CANVAS)
MONOCHROME INVERTED (STREETWEAR MOCKUP)

DESIGN CONCEPT & STORY

Sundar Bros. required an iconic monogram built with extreme optical weight. By interlocking the 'S' and 'B' letterforms inside a rounded capsule container, the mark achieves high industrial permanence and timeless prestige.

The thick line weight ensures zero loss of visual impact when embossed on heavy leather jackets, cast in metal buckles, or rendered as a micro app icon.

APPLICATION SPECS

  • Discipline: Interlocking Capsule Monogram
  • Stroke Weight: 14px unified visual line weight
  • Corner Geometry: 20px continuous corner radius
  • Primary Use: Heavy outerwear hardware, leather deboss & signage
