IMAGOTYPE

KACTUS

Architectural interior brand identity combining silhouette doorway archways with desert botanical symbolism and elegant editorial typography.

CATEGORY Architectural Imagotype
ROLE Brand Identity & Typography
TIMELINE 2024
TOOLS Illustrator, InDesign
ORIGINAL IMAGOTYPE (LIGHT CANVAS)
MONOCHROME INVERTED (STREETWEAR MOCKUP)

DESIGN CONCEPT & STORY

Kactus represents a serene fusion between brutalist architecture and organic desert flora. The heavy arch doorway cutout symbolizes entrance into curated natural living spaces.

Paired with custom tall serif letterforms, the identity speaks to minimalist interior design, architectural furniture, and bio-climatic habitat design.

APPLICATION SPECS

  • Discipline: Architectural Imagotype & Wordmark
  • Geometry: Roman archway gateway proportion system
  • Typography: Custom flared condensed serif typography
  • Primary Use: Architectural signage, linen emboss & brand collateral
