IMAGOTYPE

AUSTIN KRABS

Artisanal seafood culinary mark pairing woodblock crab illustration with bold stencil editorial typography and noble dining heritage.

CATEGORY Culinary Imagotype
ROLE Illustration & Brand System
TIMELINE 2024
TOOLS Illustrator, Photoshop
ORIGINAL IMAGOTYPE (LIGHT CANVAS)
MONOCHROME INVERTED (STREETWEAR MOCKUP)

DESIGN CONCEPT & STORY

Austin Krabs redefines coastal culinary identities by combining raw linocut anatomical wildlife craft with high-fashion editorial typography.

The bold contrast between the geometric stencil 'AUSTIN' and dynamic italicized 'KRABS' delivers immediate personality suited for restaurant facade signage, menu wax seals, and merchandise.

APPLICATION SPECS

  • Discipline: Linocut Wildlife Imagotype
  • Typography: Custom editorial bold sans + italicized serif pairing
  • Illustration: Symmetrical woodcut crab anatomy
  • Primary Use: Restaurant branding, kraft packaging & apparel
