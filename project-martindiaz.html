IMAGOTYPE

MARTIN DIAZ

Nautical sportfishing apparel brand featuring expressive gestural script calligraphy, realistic marlin swordfish linework, and vintage sea-heritage insignia.

CATEGORY Nautical Imagotype
ROLE Script Calligraphy & Illustration
TIMELINE 2024
TOOLS Photoshop, Illustrator
ORIGINAL IMAGOTYPE (LIGHT CANVAS)
MONOCHROME INVERTED (STREETWEAR MOCKUP)

DESIGN CONCEPT & STORY

Martin Diaz was created to celebrate ocean angling culture with hand-drawn warmth and high-performance coastal streetwear sensibilities.

Combining rapid brush strokes with a streamlined aerodynamic marlin profile creates a mark that communicates agility, power, and seafaring craftsmanship.

APPLICATION SPECS

  • Discipline: Script Imagotype & Coastal Lockup
  • Hand-lettering: Custom digital calligraphy script with dynamic flourishes
  • Illustration: Silhouette marlin anatomical curve study
  • Primary Use: Sun hoodies, hats, boat decals & tackle luggage
