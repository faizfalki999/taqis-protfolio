LOGOTYPE

NILTRUM

Modular isometric logotype and curated system identity engineered with multi-line dimensional typography, architectural precision, and brutalist streetwear influence.

CATEGORY Custom Logotype
ROLE Typography & Identity System
TIMELINE 2025
TOOLS Illustrator, After Effects
ORIGINAL LOGOTYPE (LIGHT CANVAS)
MONOCHROME INVERTED (STREETWEAR MOCKUP)

DESIGN CONCEPT & STORY

Niltrum is an exercise in isometric spatial typography. Built on an 8-column architectural grid, each character contains triple-line contours simulating offset extrusion depths while maintaining maximum readability at micro scales.

The stark contrast between the heavy uppercase header and the ultra-tracked geometric subtitle creates immediate visual hierarchy, suited for industrial hardware, editorial book covers, and futuristic digital interfaces.

APPLICATION SPECS

  • Discipline: Multi-line Display Logotype
  • Grid Structure: 45° isometric parallel contouring
  • Tracking: Extreme letter-spacing on secondary system type
  • Primary Use: Modular industrial hardware, tech apparatus & motion identity
