ISOTYPE

LATSOL

Aggressive solar flare sunburst emblem featuring a piercing central eye, designed for vanguard fashion labels, occult streetwear, and kinetic visual identities.

CATEGORY Occult Isotype
ROLE Symbol Design & Vector Art
TIMELINE 2025
TOOLS Illustrator, After Effects
ORIGINAL ISOTYPE (LIGHT CANVAS)
MONOCHROME INVERTED (STREETWEAR MOCKUP)

DESIGN CONCEPT & STORY

Latsol channels the raw energy of underground streetwear and occult symbolism. Designed with knife-sharp spikes radiating outward at varied geometric angles, it evokes both cosmic solar flares and protective talismans.

The central negative-space eye grounds the mark, providing an unmistakable focal point that translates into metallic screenprints, woven labels, and animated vector assets.

APPLICATION SPECS

  • Discipline: Radial Flare Isotype
  • Spike Array: 12 asymmetric directional primary & secondary vertices
  • Optical Center: Geometric almond curvature eye icon
  • Primary Use: Streetwear screenprinting, metal hardware & album artwork
