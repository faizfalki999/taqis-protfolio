IMAGOTYPE

EIE ANIMAL CARE

Erebus Isen Eldora — a heraldic fantasy crest featuring twin gargoyle dragons flanking a sacred four-point star cross, created for specialized sanctuary care.

CATEGORY Heraldic Imagotype
ROLE Crest Illustration & Identity
TIMELINE 2024
TOOLS Illustrator, Photoshop
ORIGINAL IMAGOTYPE (LIGHT CANVAS)
MONOCHROME INVERTED (STREETWEAR MOCKUP)

DESIGN CONCEPT & STORY

EIE Animal Care blends high-gothic heraldry with protective wildlife symbolism. The symmetrical twin beast composition symbolizes guardianship, loyalty, and ancient medical craftsmanship.

Constructed with strict bilateral symmetry, every angle aligns to a central vertical axis, ensuring flawless reproduction on medical seals, heavy stamps, and apparel.

APPLICATION SPECS

  • Discipline: Symmetrical Heraldic Crest
  • Symbolism: Twin winged gargoyles & central sanctuary star
  • Typography: Tracking-extended monumental sans-serif
  • Primary Use: Official sanctuary crests, packaging wax & uniform badges
